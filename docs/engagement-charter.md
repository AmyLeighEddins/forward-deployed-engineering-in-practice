| Field | Commitment |
|---|---|

| **Engineer** | Amy Eddins |
| **Outcome** | Currently the policy and many other documents are not organized in a way where sales and operations can find info easily (assumed) and it slows them down. Our goal is to create this agent that they can ask questions to and get info from since the leads (assumingly) don't have time to answer all the time. Our aim is to speed up their workflow from waiting on a response from the policy or knowledge owner to just a chat with Ask Halyard. Measured by average time to get a response multiplied by how many queries to get their needed answer. 
| **First users** | Sales and operations staff after thorough testing. The UAT testing group in the beginning (assumed since we always do UAT first). |
| **Not in scope** | Assumed it will not be making changes and it will not be giving legal advice. |
| **Decision rights** | The Security team lead (assumed) approves our access and its access and the sponsor approves scope. |
| **Evidence gate** | As we progress the SME (or equivalent role that exists) will review the test questions and answers and including tests for permission leaks. |
| **Escalation** | An access-control failure would halt the release until fixed. |
| **Open assumption** | Current policies have a named authoritative owner so that we have a way to confirm or deny validity. |
